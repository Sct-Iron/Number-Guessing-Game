How to Play

🎯 Your Goal: Guess the secret number hidden between 1 and 100.

🧩 You have 4 attempts to find the correct number — so choose wisely!

🔍 After each guess, you’ll receive a hint:

  • 🔥 Very close — within 5 numbers
  • ♨️ Warm — within 10 numbers
  • ❄️ Cold — within 20 numbers
  • 🌌 Far — Not close at all, more than 20 numbers away

⬆️ You’ll also be told whether to guess higher or lower.

📊 Track your Stats to see wins, losses, and your W/L ratio — or hit Restart Game anytime to play again!
